    Our Story

    A mom & pop staple in Archangelos

    Pizza d'oro Savas is a beloved family-run restaurant where every pizza comes from a real wood oven and every guest is treated like family. Locals and travelers alike come for the rustic atmosphere, generous portions, and pizza that gets better every day you visit.

    Whether you've had two weeks of Greek food and crave an authentic Italian-style pizza, or you're a regular who stops by three days in a row — you'll find fairly priced plates, warm hospitality, and flavors worth the trip.

    • Family operated since day one
    • Authentic wood-oven baking
    • Rustic, welcoming atmosphere
    • Fair prices · €10–15 per person
